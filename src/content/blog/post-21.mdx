**1.** Sometimes, when you try to go on YouTube to search for a topic you are learning in class- you don't find the explanation you're looking for but if you go to Settings > General > Language and change the language to Spanish, Korean, etc. you will find a whole world of videos that are in different languages that you can download and translate to understand. 

**2.** If you are going to office hours, make sure that the questions you have are ones you have reviewed and actively thought about right before office hours- not a day before, right before office hours.
